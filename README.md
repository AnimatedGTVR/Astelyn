# ✦ Astelyn

**Open. Private. Yours.**

Astelyn is a free, source-available communication platform for friends and communities, built around privacy, safety, customization, and user freedom.

Chat, call, share, create communities, and make Astelyn yours — without premium tiers or paywalled features.

## ✨ What is Astelyn?

Astelyn is being built as a modern alternative to community communication platforms such as Discord.

Our goals are simple:

* 🆓 **Free by design** — no Nitro-style subscription or premium features.
* 🔐 **Privacy focused** — private conversations should actually be private.
* 🛡️ **Safety first** — strong moderation, reporting, anti-spam, and community safety tools.
* 🎨 **Customization for everyone** — profiles, themes, animated avatars, banners, emoji, and more aren't paywalled.
* 🔎 **Source available** — Astelyn's source can be inspected, studied, modified, and contributed to.
* 💻 **Cross-platform** — Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Linux, and Web.

## 🌌 Public means public. Private means private.

Astelyn doesn't make misleading privacy promises.

Community and public channels are server-readable so communities can be properly moderated and abuse can be investigated.

Conversations designated as end-to-end encrypted are designed so that their contents remain private between their participants.

We want users to always understand **who can access what they send.**

## 💫 No premium tier

Astelyn doesn't sell basic communication and customization back to you.

Features such as:

* HD streaming
* High-quality voice and video
* Animated avatars
* Profile banners
* Profile themes
* Custom emoji
* Larger file uploads
* Community customization

are normal Astelyn features.

## 🛡️ Safety

Astelyn is intended to be a safe platform for general communities.

Pornographic and sexually explicit content, sexual exploitation, grooming, predatory behavior, and communities built around such content are not permitted on the official Astelyn service.

Moderation and safety tooling are being designed as core parts of the platform rather than afterthoughts.

## 🚧 Development Status

Astelyn is currently in **early development**.

Expect major changes, unfinished features, broken builds, and rapidly changing APIs while the foundation is being developed.

Astelyn is **not yet ready for everyday use.**
